# crownland-explorer-privacy

Privacy policy and support pages for **Crown Land Explorer: Ontario**, an independent, unofficial iOS app for finding Ontario Crown land and understanding the published rules for camping, hunting and target shooting.

Published by [BrewByte](https://github.com/brew-byte).

## View the Policy
View the hosted version: [brew-byte.github.io/crownland-explorer-privacy](https://brew-byte.github.io/crownland-explorer-privacy/)

Support: [brew-byte.github.io/crownland-explorer-privacy/support](https://brew-byte.github.io/crownland-explorer-privacy/support)

## Summary

Crown Land Explorer does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to BrewByte. To show land information, the app sends map coordinates to the Government of Ontario's public map service; maps and search are provided by Apple. No analytics, no tracking, no accounts, no ads.

## Questions?

If you have questions or concerns about the privacy policy, please [open an issue](https://github.com/Brew-Byte/crownland-explorer-privacy/issues).

## Affiliation

Crown Land Explorer is an independent, third-party app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the Government of Ontario, the Ministry of Natural Resources, or Ontario Parks.
