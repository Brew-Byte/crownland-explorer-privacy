# Privacy Policy

**Crown Land Explorer: Ontario**  
Published by BrewByte  
Last updated: October 28, 2026

## Overview

Crown Land Explorer is an independent, unofficial iPhone app that helps you find Crown land in Ontario and understand the published rules for camping, hunting and target shooting there. It uses public open data from the Government of Ontario. This policy explains what data the app does and does not collect.

## Data Collection

**We do not collect, store, or receive any personal data.**

Specifically:

- **No analytics or tracking** — the app contains no analytics SDKs, no crash reporting services, and no advertising frameworks.
- **No account required** — there is no sign-up or sign-in.
- **No BrewByte servers** — the app never sends data to BrewByte. We have no way to see where you look, what you search, or what you save.
- **No third-party SDKs** — the app uses only Apple's built-in frameworks.

## Location

If you tap **My location**, the app asks for permission to use your location **while the app is open**. It uses your location only to centre the map on where you are. Your location is never stored by the app and is never sent to BrewByte. The app never uses your location in the background.

If you don't allow location access, everything else still works: you can move the map, search for places, or enter coordinates.

## Map Look-ups

To show land information, the app has to ask public map services about the area on screen:

- **Government of Ontario (Land Information Ontario)** — the app sends the map area you're viewing, or the coordinates of a spot you tap, search for or open from your saved places, to the government's public map service. This is how it gets Crown land, park and land-use policy information. The request contains map coordinates only, not your name or any device identifier. Like any website, the government's servers may keep standard request logs (such as IP address), under the Government of Ontario's own privacy policies.
- **Apple Maps** — base maps, place search and directions are provided by Apple, under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Local Storage

The app stores the following data only on your device:

- **Saved places** — the name, coordinates and a summary of any spot you choose to save. You can delete saved places in the app at any time.
- **Preferences** — your map layer choices, and that you have accepted the disclaimer.
- **Temporary map data** — recently viewed government map data is cached for a few hours so the map loads faster.

This data never leaves your device and is removed when you delete the app.

## Permissions

The app requests the following device permission:

- **Location (While Using the App)** — only when you tap **My location**, used only to centre the map. Never requested at launch, and never used in the background.

## Your Choices

- **Location** — you can turn location access off at any time in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Crown Land**. The app keeps working without it.
- **Saved places** — swipe to delete any saved place in the app.
- **Delete everything** — deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Because BrewByte holds no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on request.

## Sharing and Sale of Data

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with anyone. We don't have any to share.

## Security

All requests to the Government of Ontario's map service are sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. Data stored by the app stays on your device and is protected by iOS.

## Links to Other Websites

The app links to official websites, such as ontario.ca, the RCMP, and land-use policy reports. Those websites are not operated by BrewByte and have their own privacy policies.

## Affiliation

Crown Land Explorer is an independent, third-party app developed by BrewByte. It is not made, affiliated with, or endorsed by the Government of Ontario, the Ministry of Natural Resources, Ontario Parks, any municipality, or the RCMP. Map data is used under the [Open Government Licence – Ontario](https://www.ontario.ca/page/open-government-licence-ontario).

## Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children and does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.
