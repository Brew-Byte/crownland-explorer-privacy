# Support

**Crown Land Explorer: Ontario**  
Published by BrewByte

## Get Help or Send Feedback

Found a problem, a mistake in the map, or have a suggestion? Please [open an issue](https://github.com/Brew-Byte/crownland-explorer-privacy/issues/new) on GitHub. Include:

- the app version (shown in **About this app**)
- the spot's coordinates, if it's about a specific place
- what you expected to see, and what you saw

## Common Questions

**Does the app tell me where camping, hunting or shooting is legal?**  
No. It shows where Crown land is mapped and summarises what published land-use policy says, with links to the official sources. It is not legal advice and does not grant permission. Always check the current rules, seasons, by-laws and posted signs before you go.

**Why is an area grey, amber or teal instead of green?**  
Green is shown only where someone has reviewed the area's own policy report. Most areas haven't been reviewed yet, so they show what still needs checking.

**Why does the map take a while to load?**  
The data comes live from the Government of Ontario's public map service, which can be slow the first time an area is viewed.

**Why does no spot show as approved for target shooting?**  
No official source marks specific Crown land spots as approved for target shooting, so the app never shows one. Check with the area's Ministry of Natural Resources office and your Chief Firearms Officer.

## Not an Official App

Crown Land Explorer is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Government of Ontario. For official answers, contact the Ministry of Natural Resources, or see [Camping on Crown land](https://www.ontario.ca/page/camping-crown-land).
